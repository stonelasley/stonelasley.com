# stonelasley.com

Stone Lasley's personal website: a portfolio of his development work and a home for content about his interests, some of it shared privately with coaching clients.

## Content

**Content Area**:
A top-level subject the site publishes in: Recipes, Watches, AI & Development, or Training.
_Avoid_: Category, section, blog (when meaning one area)

**Post**:
A single published piece of content in one Content Area, with a title, slug, date, tags, and body.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, page

**Draft**:
A Post that is written but not yet published, so readers cannot see it.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, WIP

**Recipe**:
A Post in the Recipes area that also carries structured cooking details such as times, servings, and ingredients.

**Watch**:
A Post in the Watches area about one Reference, carrying structured specs for it. Whether Stone owns an example is irrelevant to the Post.
_Avoid_: Watch catalog entry (no catalog exists yet)

**Reference**:
A manufacturer's model number identifying one watch design, such as Rolex 16610. Close variants of it may be covered by the same Watch.
_Avoid_: Model (when meaning the number), SKU

## Access

**Client**:
A coaching client of Stone's who has been granted access to private Training content.
_Avoid_: User, member, customer

**Audience**:
Who may read a Post: Public (anyone), Clients (every Client), or one named Client.
_Avoid_: Visibility, permission, role

**Private Post**:
A Post whose Audience is anything other than Public. Never appears in search.
_Avoid_: Protected content, members-only

**Access Code**:
A secret Stone issues to a Client that unlocks the Posts whose Audience includes that Client. A Client holds at most one active Access Code at a time.
_Avoid_: Password, login
