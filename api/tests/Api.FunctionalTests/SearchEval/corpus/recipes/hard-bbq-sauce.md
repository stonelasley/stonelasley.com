Discover the perfect balance of flavor with this Hard BBQ Sauce recipe! In just 25 minutes, you can create a rich and tangy sauce that elevates any grilled dish. With simple ingredients combined to achieve a smooth texture, this sauce simmers to perfection, allowing you to customize the sweetness and acidity to match your personal taste. Store it in the fridge and enjoy its deliciousness for up to three weeks—ideal for your next BBQ or family gathering!

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Whisk until smooth and uniform.
3. Simmer gently for 15–20 minutes until thickened, stirring often.
4. Taste and adjust sweetness or acidity to preference.
5. Cool fully, then store in a sealed glass jar for up to 3 weeks in the refrigerator.
