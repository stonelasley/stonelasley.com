Baked to perfection at 350°F, it makes an excellent side dish or a standalone snack. ## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish or line it with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together **avocado oil**, **Greek yogurt**, and **Splenda** until smooth.
3. Add **eggs and egg whites**, beating until fully combined.
4. Stir in **creamed corn**, **green chiles**, **cheese**, and **cottage cheese**.
5. In a separate bowl, mix **oat flour**, **cornmeal**, **whey protein**, **baking powder**, and **salt**.
6. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until just combined — do not overmix.
7. Pour batter into the prepared dish and bake for **40–45 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
8. Let cool slightly before slicing and serving.
