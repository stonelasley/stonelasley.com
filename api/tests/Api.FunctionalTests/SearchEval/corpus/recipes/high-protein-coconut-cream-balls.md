Discover the delightful taste of High-Protein Coconut Cream Balls, a perfect snack for health enthusiasts! With a blend of creamy coconut and zesty orange juice, these no-bake treats are not only easy to make but also pack a powerful protein punch. Ideal for a post-workout boost or a quick energy fix, each bite is rolled in luscious shredded coconut, making them as visually appealing as they are delicious. Enjoy guilt-free snacking with this simple recipe that yields 30 satisfying balls, perfect for sharing or indulging on your own.

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine protein powder, flour, and Splenda.
2. Add coconut oil (or cream cheese) and orange juice. Mix until a soft dough forms.
3. Stir in vanilla extract and salt if using.
4. Roll dough into 1-inch balls.
5. Roll each ball in shredded coconut to coat.
6. Chill in refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to firm.
