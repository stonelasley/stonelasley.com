## **1. General Overview**

- **Model:** Rolex Submariner Ref. 5513
- **Production:** 1962 – 1989
- **Movement:**
    - Cal. 1530 (non-chronometer, non-hacking, early)
    - Cal. 1520 (hacking from ~1972)
- **Case:** 40 mm stainless steel Oyster, identical dimensions to 5512 but non-COSC.
- **Depth rating:** 200 m = 660 ft
- **Purpose:** Professional diver, non-chronometer, tool-watch configuration.

## **12. Restoration Priorities**

1. Secure and authenticate dial first; co-branded originals are irreplaceable.
2. Match tritium hue between dial and hands.
3. Preserve chamfers and brushing; avoid over-polish.
4. Retain original insert and crystal if serviceable.
5. Document provenance rigorously for any co-signed or issued example.

# 5513 COMEX

## General Overview

- **Introduction & Context**: The Submariner Ref. 5513 was introduced in 1962 as a no-date professional dive watch. In the early 1970s, Rolex supplied a limited number to COMEX (Compagnie Maritime d’Expertises) for deep-sea testing.
- **Production Period**: Civilian 5513 ran 1962–1989; COMEX-issued examples circa 1970–1977.
- **Movement**: Calibre 1520 or 1530 (non-chronometer)
- **Case**: 40 mm stainless steel, acrylic crystal, 200 m WR; some COMEX examples modified with a helium escape valve (HEV).
- **Bezel**: Black aluminium insert, 60-minute scale, bidirectional early / unidirectional later.
- **Bracelet**: Oyster steel bracelet, references 7206/9315.
- **Significance**: Represents Rolex’s direct collaboration with professional saturation divers. The first Submariners ever fitted with a helium escape valve.

## Serial Numbers / Production Eras

| Serial Range | Years     | Notes                                                                           |
| ------------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ~2.3M–3.9M   | 1970–1977 | Period for COMEX-issue 5513s; earliest HEV test units appear within this range. |

- Quantities extremely low. Many watches destroyed or retired by COMEX.

## External Components Breakdown

| Component | Specification                                                 | Authenticity Notes                                                      |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Case      | Standard 5513 Oyster case, some with HEV drilled at 9 o’clock | Confirm valve position and finish; aftermarket modifications common.    |
| Caseback  | Engraved “ROLEX COMEX” + issue number                         | Font, depth, and spacing must match known originals.                    |
| Crystal   | Acrylic (T-19)                                                | Sapphire replacements non-original.                                     |
| Bezel     | Aluminium black insert                                        | Verify font, wear consistent with age.                                  |
| Bracelet  | Oyster 7206/9315                                              | Check period clasp stamps; later 93150 replacements reduce originality. |

## Dial Variations

| Dial Type        | Description                                         | Lume                        |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| COMEX logo matte | Black matte dial with “COMEX” print above 6 o’clock | Radium early, tritium later |
| Non-logo COMEX   | Standard dial; only caseback engraved “COMEX”       | Tritium                     |

- COMEX logo font, size, and placement are key authenticity markers.
- Service dials (gloss) post-1980 are non-original.

## Bezel / Insert Variants

- Standard black insert, serif font for 1970s period.
- Faded or “ghost” inserts accepted if patina consistent.
- No COMEX-specific insert design.

## Hands, Movement & Technical Features

- **Hands**: Mercedes hour, pencil minute, lollipop or plain seconds.
- **Movement**: Calibre 1520/1530, 17–20 jewels, 19,800–21,600 vph, 42 h power reserve.
- **HEV**: Some units factory-fitted helium escape valve, precursor to the Sea-Dweller.
- **Hacking seconds** introduced in late production.

## Part Numbers

- Movement: 1520 / 1530 (Rolex in-house)
- Case & Bracelet: Standard 5513 references; COMEX engraving adds issuance identifier only.

## Authentication & Restoration Checklist

- Verify COMEX caseback engraving style and issue number alignment with serial range.
- Confirm dial print quality and correct tritium tone.
- Inspect HEV presence and machining—original valves have factory finish and gasket seat.
- Cross-check bracelet code (year/clasp) with production year.
- Avoid heavy polishing; retain lug bevels and case geometry.
- Service interval: every 4–6 years with pressure test.

### Common Red Flags

- Aftermarket COMEX dials or casebacks.
- Standard 5513 retrofitted with non-original HEV.
- Over-polished or re-engraved cases.

## Summary

The Rolex Submariner 5513 COMEX is among the most historically important dive watches, bridging early Submariner design and the development of the Sea-Dweller. Genuine examples—especially HEV-equipped units with verified COMEX provenance—are exceedingly rare. Original engravings, period-correct tritium matte dials, and intact case geometry are essential to authenticity and collector value.
