# Rolex Oyster Perpetual Ref. 1500

## General overview

- **Model**: Oyster Perpetual Date, smooth‑bezel 34 mm. Introduced c. 1962. Produced through the late 1970s. Stainless steel was standard; related refs cover other bezels/metals. [Millenary Watches](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-1500/)
- **Production period**: c. 1962–late 1970s (serials present into 1978–79). [oysterworld.de](https://www.oysterworld.de/http/-/oysterworld-de/production-dates-html/)
- **Key specs**
    - **Case**: 34 mm steel Oyster, Twinlock screw‑down crown, 19 mm lugs. Approx. 11.5–12 mm thick. 100 m (10 ATM) period rating. Acrylic Cyclops crystal. Smooth bezel. [Millenary Watches+2Zeit Vintage Watch+2](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-1500/)
    - **Movement**: Rolex Cal. 1565 (early) then 1570/1575 family (date variant), automatic, no quick‑set; hacking added early 1970s. [Beckertime, LLC+2Millenary Watches+2](https://beckertime.com/blog/review-of-the-rolex-date-ref-1500/)
    - **Bracelet**: Oyster rivet 7205 (19 mm), folded 7835 (19 mm), later solid 78350 (19 mm); Jubilee options existed. [Rolex Forums+3eBay+3Chrono24+3](https://www.ebay.com/itm/157366182301)
- **Why it matters**: The ref. 1500 is the archetypal mid‑size chronometer Date using the robust 15xx calibres, often the most accessible entry to COSC‑rated vintage Rolex. [Bobswatches.com](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/watch-review/vintage-week-rolex-oyster-perpetual-chronometer-date-ref-1500.html)

## Bezel / insert / external variants (same case family)

- **1500**: smooth steel bezel (this dossier). **1501**: steel engine‑turned. **1503**: 14 k fluted. **1505**: two‑tone engine‑turned. **1507**: 18 k fluted. Rare: **1502** (Florentine), **1504** (geometric). [Millenary Watches+1](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-1500/)

_Counterfeit risk_: swapping 1501 engine‑turned bezels onto 1500; verify reference between lugs and bezel type. [Millenary Watches](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-1500/)

## Hands, movement & technical features

- **Hands**: Baton with tritium; early 1960 examples documented with dauphine/arrow furniture. Date discs with open 6/9 and “hook‑7” seen mid‑60s. [vintagewatchcentre.com+1](https://www.vintagewatchcentre.com/product/rolex-oyster-perpetual-model-ref-1500-1960/)
- **Cal. 1565 (early)**: 26 j, 18,000 vph, free‑sprung balance with overcoil, instantaneous date, non‑hacking. [Beckertime, LLC+1](https://beckertime.com/blog/review-of-the-rolex-date-ref-1500/)
- **Cal. 1570/1575 (date variant)**: 19,800 vph, 25–26 j, Microstella free‑sprung, ~48 h reserve, hacking added ~1971/72; no quick‑set. Some plates stamped “1570” though functionally 1575 with date. [Millenary Watches+2Millenary Watches+2](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-caliber-1575-complete-guide/)
- **Water resistance**: Oyster case era rating 100 m; treat vintage examples cautiously. [Chrono24](https://www.chrono24.co.uk/rolex/ref-1500.htm)

## Authentication & restoration checklist

**Originality checks**

- **Between lugs**: “1500” at 12, serial at 6; inspect font and corrosion. [qualitytyme.net](https://qualitytyme.net/serial-numbers/)
- **Case‑back**: Inside ref and quarter‑year code pre‑1972; mismatches are common but note plausibility. [rolexhaven.com](https://rolexhaven.com/case-back-codes.html)
- **Movement**: Correct 15xx bridge engravings; era‑correct (1565 early, 1570/1575 later). Hacking behavior consistent with c. 1971/72 onward. [Beckertime, LLC+1](https://beckertime.com/blog/review-of-the-rolex-date-ref-1500/)
- **Dial**: Lume line vs era (“SWISS”→“T SWISS T/SWISS T<25”; σ‑dials early 1970s). Watch for Luminova service dials marked “SWISS” (1998–99) or “SWISS MADE.” [rolexpassionreport.com+2Hodinkee+2](https://rolexpassionreport.com/15606/the-evolution-of-rolex-luminous/)
- **Date wheel**: Period fonts (open 6/9, hook‑7 mid‑60s). [watchclub.com](https://www.watchclub.com/rolex/oyster-date/vintage1966beautifulexample-ref-1500-year-1965)
- **Bracelet**: 19 mm width; verify bracelet ref, end‑link code (57/357/557) and clasp date vs case serial. [eBay+2Chrono24+2](https://www.ebay.com/itm/157366182301)

**Common red flags**

- Engine‑turned/fluted bezels on a case stamped 1500. [Millenary Watches](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-1500/)
- 20 mm end‑links on 19 mm lugs; aftermarket crystal not 25‑117; modern crowns mis‑matched. [Chrono24+1](https://www.chrono24.co.uk/rolex/braceletstrap-vintage-exc5-rolex-oyster-7835-end-links-357-stainless-steel-19mm-bracelet--id43310389.htm)
- Tritium dial relume with Luminova without “Swiss”/“Swiss Made” change. [Watch Chest](https://watchchest.com/journal/from-radium-to-chromalight.html)

**Restoration guidance**

- **Sympathetic**: Light case refresh only; retain chamfers and lug holes. Use OEM **25‑117** crystal and period‑correct hands if replaced. [timeconnectioninc.com](https://www.timeconnectioninc.com/products/genuine-cyclop-crystal-25-117-to-fit-rolex)
- **Bracelets**: Period‑matching 7205/7835/78350 or 6251H with correct 19 mm ends. [eBay+2Chrono24+2](https://www.ebay.com/itm/157366182301)
- **Service interval**: Regular service for 15xx (~5–10 years) to preserve chronometer performance; avoid pressure testing to modern specs on original crowns/tubes. _(General practice; confirm with your watchmaker.)_
- **Provenance**: Note co‑signed dials (Serpico y Laino; UAE crests); keep clasp/dial/movement photos and any RSC paperwork. [sothebys.com+1](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2020/watches-weekly-new-york-9/rolex-reference-1500-date-retailed-by-serpico-y)

## Summary

Ref. 1500 combines the classic 34 mm Oyster case with Rolex’s 15xx automatic architecture. Early pieces carry Cal. 1565; later watches use 1570/1575 with hacking from ~1971/72. Dials transition from early gilt/“SWISS” to tritium “T SWISS T,” with sigma dials in the 1970s. Correct crystals (25‑117), 19 mm bracelets and end‑links (57/357/557), and period fonts on the date wheel are key tells. It is a durable, chronometer‑rated vintage Rolex with broad parts support and strong collector liquidity. [Chrono24+4Beckertime, LLC+4Millenary Watches+4](https://beckertime.com/blog/review-of-the-rolex-date-ref-1500/)

### Verification notes (conflicts resolved)

- **Hacking start**: Sources cite 1971–72. For dating, treat 2.59–3.05 M serials (1971–72) as the changeover window. [gmtmasterhistory.com+1](https://gmtmasterhistory.com/gmt-master_ref_1675.html)
- **1570 vs 1575 stamping**: Some “1570” bridges power date models; functionally this is the 1575 variant. Cross‑check by calendar works and case period. [Millenary Watches](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-caliber-1570-a-complete-guide/)
