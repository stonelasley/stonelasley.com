# Rolex Cosmograph Daytona ref. 6265

## General Overview

- **Model**: Oyster Cosmograph “Daytona” ref. 6265 (metal engraved tachymeter bezel; screw-down pushers).
- **Introduction / Production**: Presented c.1969 in Rolex literature; regular production from 1971; discontinued 1987–1988. ([Phillips](https://www.phillips.com/detail/rolex/194917))
- **Case**: 37–37.5 mm, Oyster steel or 14k/18k yellow gold; screw-down crown and pushers; acrylic crystal. Typical lug width 19 mm. ([Sothebys.com](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2024/important-watches-part-i-2/daytona-big-red-reference-6265-a-stainless-steel))
- **Water resistance (period rating)**: Improved over pump-pusher models; period sources note 50 m early moving to 100 m on later Triplock-equipped examples. Treat vintage pieces as non-waterproof unless tested. ([OfficialWatches](https://www.officialwatches.com/blogs/articles/rolex-daytona-6265))
- **Movement**: Rolex cal. 727 (Rolex-finished Valjoux 72 family), 17 jewels, 21,600 vph, ~48 h PR, column wheel, horizontal clutch, Breguet overcoil, Microstella regulation. ([WatchBase.com](https://watchbase.com/rolex/caliber/727))
- **Complications**: 3-register chronograph (30-min, 12-h, running seconds); tachymeter bezel.
- **Bezel**: Solid metal (steel or gold) engraved **50–200 units/hour**; multiple font “Mk” variants; rare early anomalies with 300 UPH reported. ([41Watch](https://www.41watch.com/en/journal/buyers-guides/rolex-cosmograph-daytona-6265-big-red-fiche-technique))
- **Crystal**: Acrylic (Rolex “25-21” / Tropic 21). ([eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/405827932100))
- **Bracelet**: Early folded-link Oyster 7835 (19 mm); later solid-link 78350 (19 mm). Gold pieces often on riveted 7205 with “71” end-links. ([Rolex Forums](https://www.rolexforums.com/showthread.php?t=911639))
- **Why it matters**: Final, longest-running manual-wind Oyster Daytona with screw-down pushers; canonical “Big Red” dials; broadest parts standardization before the automatic era. ([Sothebys.com](https://www.sothebys.com/en/articles/how-to-start-collecting-the-rolex-daytona))

## Bezel / Insert / External Variants

- **Metal bezel, 200 UPH**: default; Mk 1–3 font evolutions (spacing at 60/200, fonts on “2/6/9”), plus Rolex **service** variant. Higher desirability for original Mk correct to serial era. ([41Watch](https://www.41watch.com/en/journal/buyers-guides/rolex-cosmograph-daytona-6265-big-red-fiche-technique))
- **300 UPH**: occasionally seen; usually incorrect for 6265; investigate case history and parts swaps. ([Timezone Forums](https://forums.timezone.com/index.php?t=msg&th=2173898))
- **Counterfeits**: soft edges, wrong enamel fills, wrong font at “200”. Compare to known originals. ([Chrono24](https://www.chrono24.com/rolex/bezel-daytona-bezel-mk1--original-invoice---62396240624162656262--id43127901.htm))

## Hands, Movement & Technical Features

- **Hands**: Straight baton with tritium; sub-register hands polished or black-painted depending on dial. Early/late shapes are consistent; relumes are common.
- **Pushers**: Mk I→Mk II (**P301**), later **P302**; avoid 6240 “millerighe” on 6265. ([EyeOnWatch.com](https://eyeonwatch.com/rolex-watch-parts-diagram/))
- **Crowns**: Early Twinlock **24-700**; later Triplock **24-702/703**; dot signature on crown should match era. ([eBay](https://www.ebay.com/b/Rolex-Daytona-6265-In-other-Watch-Parts/10324/bn_7022507072))
- **Cal. 727**: 17j, 21,600 vph, ~48 h PR; column-wheel, horizontal clutch; Breguet overcoil; Microstella balance; non-hacking. Rolex upgrade from 18,000 vph 722/722-1. ([WatchBase.com](https://watchbase.com/rolex/caliber/727))

## Authentication & Restoration Checklist

**Originality checks**

- **Dial**: Text layout vs era (no-Daytona / Sigma / small-red / Big Red); “σ” presence; minute track and sub-dial printing; tritium plots shape and tone. ([Bobswatches.com](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/watch-review/sigma-dial-reference-6265-rolex-daytona.html))
- **Bezel**: 200 UPH and correct font for serial; avoid fresh service bezels on early pieces unless disclosed. ([41Watch](https://www.41watch.com/en/journal/buyers-guides/rolex-cosmograph-daytona-6265-big-red-fiche-technique))
- **Pushers**: P301 vs P302 by era; reject 6240 millerighe on 6265. ([41Watch](https://www.41watch.com/en/journal/historical-reviews-of-iconic-watches/retrospective-complete-de-la-rolex-daytona))
- **Crown**: Twinlock early, Triplock later; dot signature match. ([The Beautiful Watch](https://www.thebeautifulwatch.com/blogs/news/the-rolex-6265))
- **Caseback**: Inside stamping **6265**; occasional **6262** is period-correct. Date quarter stamps can help. ([Pushers](https://pushers.io/listings/1973-rolex-daytona-ref-6265-silver-roc-dial))
- **Bracelet**: 19 mm; 7835/271 early, 78350/571 later; gold 7205/71; clasp date aligns with head. ([Rolex Forums](https://www.rolexforums.com/showthread.php?t=911639))
- **Movement**: Bridge signed “ROLEX GENEVA,” cal. **727**, 17 jewels. ([Sothebys.com](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2024/important-watches-part-i-2/daytona-big-red-reference-6265-a-stainless-steel))
- **Co-branding**: Tiffany-signed dials exist; require period paperwork or retailer letter. ([EveryWatch](https://everywatch.com/rolex/daytona/6265/watch-9098877))

**Red flags**

- Fresh luminova on “tritium” dials; wrong bezel scale; mismatched pusher generation; 20 mm end-links; over-polished lugs; fantasy “exotic” dial on steel 6265 without provenance. ([41Watch](https://www.41watch.com/en/journal/buyers-guides/rolex-cosmograph-daytona-6265-big-red-fiche-technique))

**Restoration guidance**

- **Sympathetic** case work; retain chamfers.
- Source period-correct **25-21** crystal, pusher set (**P301/P302**) as appropriate, and correct bracelet/end-links. ([Time Connection II, Inc](https://www.timeconnectioninc.com/products/plastic-tropic-crystal-25-21-to-fit-rolex))
- Keep original tritium dial/hands; stabilize, do not relume.
- Service interval: 4–6 years with a vintage-savvy watchmaker; pressure-test only if use near water (generally avoid).
- Document: photos of dial, caseback, movement, bracelet codes; keep any retailer letters (e.g., Tiffany). ([EveryWatch](https://everywatch.com/rolex/daytona/6265/watch-9098877))

## Summary

Ref. **6265** is the screw-down, metal-bezel manual Daytona. Production spans c.1971–1987 with cal. **727** throughout. Collecting centers on **Sigma** and **Big Red** dials, correct **200 UPH** bezels, and era-consistent **pusher/crown** and **bracelet** sets. Co-signed dials exist and command premiums with proof. Correctness by era drives value more than condition alone. ([Christie's](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-6349665))

**Conflicts resolved**: Some catalog texts cite a 1969 debut; serial evidence and auction scholarship place regular production from **1971** and discontinuation by **1987–88**. Both are noted above. ([Phillips](https://www.phillips.com/detail/rolex/194917))
