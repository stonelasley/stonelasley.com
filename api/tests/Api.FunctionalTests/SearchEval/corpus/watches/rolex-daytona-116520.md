## 1. General & Technical Overview

- Production years: approx. **2000 to 2016** [Timeless Vintage+2OusWatch+2](https://timelessvintage.watch/blog/reference-116520%2C-the-updated-rolex-daytona)
- Case size: 40 mm, stainless steel
- Water resistance: 100 m / 330 ft [Bobswatches.com+1](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/resources/rolex-daytona-116520.html)
- Crystal: sapphire
- Bezel: fixed stainless steel tachymeter bezel (steel, engraved) [Bobswatches.com+2Timeless Vintage+2](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/resources/rolex-daytona-116520.html)
- Movement: Rolex in-house **Caliber 4130** chronograph movement [OusWatch+2Bobswatches.com+2](https://ouswatch.com/ultimate-rolex-daytona-116520-guide/)
    - Vertical clutch, column wheel
    - ~72 h power reserve (improvement over previous) [OusWatch+1](https://ouswatch.com/ultimate-rolex-daytona-116520-guide/)
    - Fewer parts than predecessors (simplified construction) [OusWatch+1](https://ouswatch.com/ultimate-rolex-daytona-116520-guide/)
- Dial options: black or white (“Panda”) configurations [Bobswatches.com+1](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/resources/rolex-daytona-116520.html)
- Bracelet: Oyster bracelet, solid links, Oysterlock clasp, with Easylink extension [OusWatch+1](https://ouswatch.com/ultimate-rolex-daytona-116520-guide/)
