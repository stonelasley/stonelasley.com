## **1. General Overview**

- **Model:** Rolex Submariner Ref. 5512
- **Production:** 1959 – 1978
- **Movement:** Chronometer-certified Caliber 1560 (early) → Caliber 1570 (late)
- **Case:** 40 mm stainless steel Oyster, chronometer-rated, crown-guard case
- **Depth rating:** 200 m = 660 ft
- **Distinct trait:** COSC-certified version of the 5513; identical architecture but regulated and adjusted to chronometer standards.

## **9. Restoration Priorities**

1. Preserve original matte dial and tritium hands; never relume unless non-functional.
2. Maintain factory chamfers and radial brushing.
3. Use genuine Tropic 19 crystal; service replacements lower collector value.
4. If movement bridges are replaced, retain original chronometer engraving.

## **2. Acquisition Order (High-Leverage First)**

1. **Correct dial** – hardest to source, most decisive for authenticity.
2. **Matching tritium hands** – visual coherence; protect dial from mismatched lume.
3. **Bezel insert** – drives aesthetic and resale value.
4. **Bracelet (9315 / 280)** – secure period correctness early; prices rising.
5. **Crystal & crown** – simpler replacements once core components secured.
6. **Movement and gaskets** – standard service items; last step in mechanical restoration.

---

## **3. Verification Checklist**

- **Dial:** Inspect under loupe for crisp white text, clean edges, and tritium that fluoresces briefly under UV then fades.
- **Insert:** Confirm metallic printing and silver tone, not bright white.
- **Bracelet:** Check clasp code matches production quarter (e.g., “3/71”).
- **Movement:** Chronometer bridge engraving must match serial range.
- **Case:** Factory bevel geometry should be symmetric; avoid re-engraved lugs.
