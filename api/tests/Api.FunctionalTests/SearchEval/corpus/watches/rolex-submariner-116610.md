### **General Overview**

- Introduction: The 116610 series was introduced in 2010 as the stainless-steel “Submariner Date” with a ceramic bezel, successor to reference 16610.
- Production period: Approximately **2010–2020**.
- Discontinuation: The series ended in 2020 and was replaced by the 41 mm case variants (ref. 126610) in the updated Submariner line.
- Key technical specifications:
    - Case material: Oystersteel (904L stainless steel)
    - Diameter: 40 mm
    - Thickness: approx. 13 mm (various sources ~13mm)
    - Water resistance: 300 m / 1000 ft.
    - Movement: calibre 3135 (automatic, date)
    - Bezel: Unidirectional diving bezel with Cerachrom (ceramic) insert (in LN = black, in LV = green)
    - Crystal: Sapphire, with Cyclops magnifier for date window.
    - Bracelet: Oyster bracelet with Glidelock extension system.
- Why this reference matters:
    - First regular steel Submariner Date in 40 mm with ceramic bezel from Rolex, marking a significant technical/material leap.
    - Collector significance: Both the black-bezel LN and the green-dial/green-bezel LV (“Hulk”) variant gained strong following.
    - Represents the end of the 40 mm era before the case size increased to 41 mm in 2020. Thus a transitional model for Rolex enthusiasts.

### **Bezel / Insert / External Variants**

- Bezel insert material: Cerachrom ceramic (scratch-resistant) – major upgrade from previous aluminium bezel inserts.
- Colour / variant list:
    - 116610LN → Black ceramic bezel with black dial.
    - 116610LV → Green ceramic bezel with green dial (nicknamed “Hulk”).
- Font variations: Early ceramic inserts may have different weights of numerals or engraving depth; subtle collector concerns.
- Approximate era: Entire production (2010–2020) uses Cerachrom in this reference. Aluminium insert variants were for predecessor 16610.
- Collector desirability & counterfeit risks:
    - LV “Hulk” is highly desirable and commands premium.
    - Counterfeit risk: non-ceramic inserts, incorrect colour green, mis-engraving, or older aluminium stamped inserts passed off as Cerachrom.
- Authentication: Check bezel insert for correct ceramic shine, no fading, correct alignment, and bezel action (120-click unidirectional) with proper feel.

### **Hands, Movement & Technical Features**

- Movement: calibre 3135 (automatic, date complication). COSC-certified.
    - Frequency: 28,800 vph (4 Hz)
    - Jewels: 31 jewels (typical for 3135) – though always verify specific batch.
    - Power reserve: ~48 hours standard (some sources mention this approximate figure)
    - Hairspring upgrade: Later batches of 3135 in this reference include Rolex’s Parachrom hairspring improving magnetic and shock resistance.
- Hands: Broad “Mercedes” hour hand, large luminous hour markers consistent with Maxi dial. In early production, minute/seconds hand proportions may slightly differ.
- Crown & protection: Triplock crown with triple seal system; crown guards larger on “Super Case”.
- No major movement change within the reference. Subsequent model (126610) upgraded movement to calibre 3235.
- Technical upgrade summary vs predecessor: Cerachrom bezel + Glidelock clasp + Super Case + Chromalight lume set.

### **Authentication & Restoration Checklist**

**Originality checks:**

- Case: Sharp chamfers, correct weighting (~155 g for full steel bracelet)
- Dial printing: Crisp, correct text spacing, correct Chromalight lume (blue glow)
- Serial number & reference: Check serial between lugs (pre-2010) or on rehaut (post-2008) and reference number on opposite lug.
- Bezel: Cerachrom insert, correct colour and engraving, aligned zero marker
- Bracelet & clasp: Solid end links, correct clasp code, Glidelock function functional
- Movement: 3135 stamped calibre, COSC certification (if visible), correct rotor and bridging finish
- Papers & box: Original warranty card, correct model/reference, box set details

**Common red flags:**

- Over-polished case (lugs become overly rounded)
- Incorrect or faded bezel insert (indicating aftermarket or replacement)
- Service dials with incorrect lume (green glow instead of blue)
- Mismatched serial/reference numbers or altered lugs
- Fake ceramic bezel (some aftermarket inserts still aluminium)
- Bracelet non-original or aftermarket clasp

**Recommended restoration practices:**

- Maintain original case finishing where possible; avoid polishing down to new-factory specs unless severe damage.
- Use genuine Rolex parts for bezel insert, crown, bracelet links if replacement needed.
- Service interval: ~5-10 years depending on use; ensure proper water-resistance re-seal after servicing.
- Document service history, keep original parts replaced and box/papers safe for provenance.
- For vintage collectors: retain original bezel insert where possible — even with minor wear this increases value relative to aftermarket parts.

### **Summary**

The Rolex Submariner Reference 116610 (2010–2020) represents a key transitional moment in Rolex’s iconic diver lineage: the final 40 mm steel Submariner Date with the “Super Case”, ceramic bezel, Glidelock bracelet, and calibre 3135 movement. Its technological upgrades over the previous generation and its discontinuation in 2020 add to its collector appeal. For authentication or restoration, careful attention to case proportions, bezel insert material, dial & lume quality, bracelet/clasp authenticity and movement stamping is essential. The ref. 116610 occupies a strong position in the modern Submariner market—robust, well known, and desirable to both tool-watch purists and luxury collectors.
