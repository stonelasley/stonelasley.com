## **1. General Overview**

- **Model:** Submariner Date Ref. 1680
- **Production:** circa 1969–1979
- **Movement:** Rolex Caliber **1575** (date version of Cal. 1570, with hacking introduced ~1972)
- **Case material:** Stainless steel, reference **5513/1680-type Oyster case** (40 mm)
- **Depth rating:** 660 ft = 200 m (earlier “Submariner” red text dials read “Submariner / 660ft = 200m”)

---

## **8. Movement Notes**

- **Caliber:** 1575 (date)
- **Jewels:** 26
- **Beat rate:** 19,800 A/h
- **Regulation:** Microstella free-sprung balance
- **Rotor bearing:** Perforated automatic bridge (full-rotor)
- **Key differences:** Post-’72 examples have hacking seconds; pre-’72 do not.

## **9. Restoration Advice**

- Retain original case chamfers and brushing direction (radial on lugs).
- Avoid polishing crown guards.
- Re-lume only if necessary for legibility; original tritium patina is collector-critical.
- Preserve period-correct Tropic 127 crystal and fat-font insert if possible.

**Rolex–Tiffany & Co. co-signed dials** appeared from roughly the **late 1950s through the mid-1990s**, with peak activity between the **1970s and mid-1980s**.

Key points by era:

| Period               | Context                                                                                                                                                               | Models Commonly Seen                                               |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Late 1950s–1960s** | Early Tiffany-signed Rolex sold exclusively through the Tiffany & Co. flagship in New York. Dials hand-stamped locally by Tiffany.                                    | Datejust 1601, Explorer 1016, Air-King 5500                        |
| **1970s**            | Formal Rolex–Tiffany retail relationship. Tiffany received blank dials from Rolex, applied its own logo (black pad-print or lacquer stamp), then cased and sold them. | Submariner 1680, GMT-Master 1675, Datejust 1603, Daytona 6263/6265 |
| **Early–Mid 1980s**  | Continuation but quality control issues arose due to inconsistent stamping and warranty handling. Rolex began restricting co-branding.                                | Submariner 16800, GMT-Master 16750, Datejust 16030                 |
| **Circa 1990–1993**  | Relationship terminated. Rolex insisted all branding remain factory-controlled. Tiffany-signed dials ceased.                                                          |                                                                    |

**Authenticity criteria:**

- Double signature printed _above 6 o’clock_ or occasionally _below “Superlative Chronometer Officially Certified”_.
- Font must align with correct period dial printing (e.g., matte red-text 1680 dials with small “Tiffany & Co.” serif font).
- Dial printing must be on top of lacquer, not under it.
- Service dials never bear the Tiffany stamp.

A **Submariner 1680 “Tiffany & Co.”** therefore would date approximately **1970–1976**, matte red or early white dial period, with genuine examples extremely scarce due to Tiffany’s small volume and Rolex’s later destruction of leftover signed dials.

# 1680 COMEX

## General Overview

- COMEX-issued pieces produced circa 1977–1979.
- **Movement**: Calibre 1575 (hacking seconds)
- **Case**: 40 mm stainless steel, acrylic crystal
- **Bezel**: Bidirectional early, unidirectional later; black aluminium insert
- **Water Resistance**: 200 m / 660 ft
- **Bracelet**: Oyster 9315 or 93150
- **Significance**: One of the earliest dated COMEX Submariners. Links professional tool-watch design with saturation diving heritage.

## Serial Numbers / Production Eras

| Serial Range | Years     | Notes                                                  |
| ------------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| ~5.40–5.50M  | 1978–1979 | Late matte dial era; COMEX-issued units observed here. |

- Quantities: Not officially disclosed by Rolex. Estimated very limited.

## External Components Breakdown

| Component | Specification                         | Authenticity Notes                           |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Case      | 40 mm Oystersteel                     | Verify correct serial range; no HEV.         |
| Caseback  | Engraved “ROLEX COMEX” + issue number | Engraving font, depth, and spacing critical. |
| Bezel     | Black aluminium, period font          | Aftermarket inserts common.                  |
| Crystal   | Acrylic T-19                          | Replace only with OEM vintage parts.         |
| Bracelet  | Oyster 9315→93150                     | Correct clasp codes for late 1970s.          |

## Dial Variations

| Dial Type        | Description                                         | Lume    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| COMEX logo matte | COMEX print above 6 o’clock, tritium “T SWISS T<25” | Tritium |
| No-logo COMEX    | Engraved back only                                  | Tritium |

- Authentic matte finish; glossy dials are service replacements.

## Bezel / Insert Variants

- Standard 1680 insert, serif font typical of period.
- No special COMEX-exclusive bezels.

## Hands, Movement & Technical Features

- **Hands**: Mercedes set, matching lume tone
- **Movement**: Calibre 1575, 19,800 vph, hacking seconds, date
- **No helium escape valve**
- **Power Reserve**: ~48 h

## Part Numbers

- Movement: 1575 (in-house)
- Case & Bracelet: Standard 1680 components; COMEX engraving adds issuance mark, not unique PN.

## Authentication & Restoration Checklist

- Verify COMEX engraving and serial within 1978–79 range.
- Dial printing and lume tone must match late-1970s matte format.
- Preserve original acrylic crystal and bezel insert if possible.
- Avoid polishing; retain factory chamfers.
- Service every 4–6 years (functional use) or 6–8 years (stored).

## Summary

The Rolex Submariner 1680 COMEX merges the first-date Submariner platform with professional COMEX issue provenance. Authentic examples combine matte tritium dials, engraved casebacks, and unmodified cases. Documentation and originality drive collector value.
