# Rolex Sea‑Dweller Ref. 126600 — Full Technical & Collector Dossier

> Model: Oyster Perpetual Sea‑Dweller “Single Red”
>
> **References covered:** 126600‑0001 (2017–c.2022), 126600‑0002 (c.2022–present)

## General Overview

**What it is.**

Launched at Baselworld **2017** for the Sea‑Dweller’s 50th anniversary, the 126600 is the first standard Sea‑Dweller to grow to **43 mm**, to re‑introduce **red “SEA‑DWELLER” text**, and—controversially for die‑hards—to add a **Cyclops** over the date. It carries the new‑generation **calibre 3235** and remains the current steel Sea‑Dweller as of November 2025. ([Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/the-rolex-sea-dweller-reference-126600))

**Key technical specs (factory).**

- **Case:** Oystersteel (904L/“Oystersteel”), **43 mm** diameter; ~**15.0–15.5 mm** thick (independent measurements); **22 mm** lugs. Helium escape valve at 9 o’clock; Triplock crown; screw‑down back. **WR 1,220 m / 4,000 ft.** ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
- **Bezel:** Unidirectional, black **Cerachrom** insert; numerals and hash‑marks **PVD‑coated with platinum**. 60‑minute dive scale. ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
- **Crystal:** Flat sapphire with **Cyclops** over date; laser‑etched coronet at 6 (LEC). ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
- **Dial:** Glossy black “Maxi” dial with blue‑emission **Chromalight**; single red “SEA‑DWELLER.” ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
- **Movement:** **Rolex 3235**; 28,800 vph; ~70 h power reserve; Chronergy escapement; Parachrom hairspring; Paraflex shocks; **–2/+2 s/day after casing** (“Superlative Chronometer”). ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
- **Bracelet / clasp:** Oyster bracelet (**ref. 97220**), Oysterlock safety clasp; **Glidelock** fine adjustment. Early production also featured an additional **Fliplock** wetsuit extension link; current steel 126600 brochure states **Glidelock** only. (Details by production era below.) ([Professional Watches](https://professionalwatches.com/baselworld-2017-rolex-sea-dweller/))

**Why it matters.**

Historically, the 126600 marks the 50‑year chapter of Rolex’s saturation‑diver lineage, blending modern materials and the 3235 with “heritage red” scripting. For collectors it is the **first Cyclops Sea‑Dweller**, the **first 43 mm Sea‑Dweller**, and the return of **red text**—three traits that define the reference’s identity and desirability. ([Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/the-rolex-sea-dweller-reference-126600))

## Bezel / Insert / External Variants

- **Insert types:** Single standard **black Cerachrom** with platinum‑filled markings (no official color or font variants). ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
- **Collector notes:** Because fonts are laser‑milled and PVD‑filled, counterfeit tells are usually color tone (too white), incorrect serif terminations, and a dull pearl. (Compare against known authentic examples from the period.)
- **Cyclops:** Present on **all 126600** variants; this is a key differentiator from the 116600. ([Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/the-rolex-sea-dweller-reference-126600))

## Hands, Movement & Technical Features

**Hands / Furniture.**

White‑gold **Mercedes** hour, paddle minute, and lollipop seconds with Chromalight; “Maxi” applied markers in WG. Date at 3 with Cyclops. ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))

**Calibre 3235 (in‑house).**

- **Type:** Self‑winding with bidirectional rotor; hacking seconds; rapid‑set date.
- **Regulation:** Free‑sprung balance with Microstella; **Parachrom** hairspring; **Paraflex** shock absorbers.
- **Escapement:** **Chronergy** (Ni‑P), increased efficiency.
- **Spec:** ~**70 h** power reserve; **28,800 vph**; **–2/+2 s/day** after casing (Rolex Superlative).
- **Service marks:** “ROLEX 3235” bridge engravings; finishing consistent with modern Professional line. ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))

**Waterproofing hardware.**

**Triplock** crown (three dots); helium escape valve; **sapphire** crystal; Oyster case architecture (monobloc middle, screw back/crown). ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))

## Authentication & Restoration Checklist

**Originality checks (head):**

- **Dial:** Correct **single red “SEA‑DWELLER”**; verify **MK1 vs MK2** at 6 o’clock (presence/absence of the tiny coronet); consistent fonts and spacing. ([The Watch Club](https://www.watchclub.com/rolex/sea-dweller/50thanniversarymodel-ref-126600-year-2019-14091))
- **Crystal:** Confirm **LEC** at 6 and properly centered Cyclops placement/magnification. ([Bobswatches.com](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/editorial/evolution-rolex-coronet.html))
- **Case:** Factory brushing and edge geometry; genuine **HEV** profile at 9; **Triplock** crown with proper gasket feel. WR engraving is not on lugs (modern Rolex).
- **Caseback:** For Sea‑Dweller, perimeter text **“ROLEX OYSTER – ORIGINAL GAS ESCAPE VALVE.”** (Avoid Deepsea‑style inner flange texts.) ([European Watch Company](https://www.europeanwatch.com/watch/Rolex-126600-Sea-Dweller-RED-43mm-50th-Anniversary-SS-2020-37881.html))
- **Rehaut / serial:** Randomized serial engraved on rehaut; alignments at 12 (coronet) and at serial position are clean and even. ([Luxury Watches USA](https://luxurywatchesusa.com/rolex-serial-number-guide-authenticity/))

**Bracelet / clasp:**

- **Reference:** **97220** correct for 126600; check for tightened tolerances, SEL fit, and bracelet codes appropriate to the delivery year. ([Millenary Watches](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-bracelet-reference-numbers/))
- **Extension systems by era:** 2017–c.2021/22 often show **Glidelock + Fliplock**; from c.2022 **Glidelock‑only** per 126600‑0002. Confirm against documentation. ([Professional Watches](https://professionalwatches.com/baselworld-2017-rolex-sea-dweller/))

**Common red flags.**

- **Aftermarket dials/inserts** with too‑white bezel numerals or “pearl” tone; misaligned minute track. ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
- **Incorrect dial mark:** MK2 crown‑at‑six on a 2017‑dated set (possible dial swap) or MK1 dials on late cards; scrutinize provenance. ([The Watch Club](https://www.watchclub.com/rolex/sea-dweller/50thanniversarymodel-ref-126600-year-2019-14091))
- **Over‑polish:** Rounded lug tips & soft crown‑guard shoulders; loss of brushing direction.
- **Wrong bracelet:** Non‑97220 bracelets or 20 mm bracelets adapted with spacers.

**Sympathetic restoration guidance.**

- **No re‑lume**: Chromalight replacement dials/hands are available only through Rolex; replacing one element may force replacement of the set—document any changes.
- **Crystals:** Replace with OEM sapphire during pressure‑test service; retain removed crystal in the file.
- **Bezel insert:** Ceramic rarely needs replacement; do **not** refinish.
- **Service interval:** Rolex advises periodic servicing; modern guidance is **~10 years**, adjusted to usage (divers should have seals/WR checked more frequently). Keep **pressure‑test** records. ([Rolex](https://www.rolex.com/watch-care-and-service/faq))
- **Documentation:** Retain **warranty card, hangtags, booklets**, and all **service receipts**. Log bracelet link count when acquired and after any adjustment.

## Summary

The **126600 Sea‑Dweller** is the modern sweet‑spot of Rolex saturation divers: a **43 mm**, **1,220 m** professional tool with the efficient **cal. 3235**, **Cerachrom** bezel and **Chromalight** legibility—made distinctive by its **single line of red text** and the debut of a **Cyclops** on a Sea‑Dweller. Early pieces (126600‑0001) carry launch cues—MK1 dials and many with **Fliplock**—while the current **126600‑0002** standardizes **Glidelock‑only** with the MK2 “crown‑at‑six” dial. For authentication, focus on dial mark, rehaut engraving, bracelet **97220**, and correct caseback legend. As a 50th‑anniversary model still in production, it combines robust daily wear with clear collector hooks rooted in Sea‑Dweller history. ([Rolex](https://assets.rolex.com/api/brochure/en-us/sea-dweller/m126600-0002.pdf))
