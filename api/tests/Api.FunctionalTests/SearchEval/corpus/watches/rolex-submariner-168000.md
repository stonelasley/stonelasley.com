## General Overview

**Model & history.** The Submariner ref. **168000** is the short‑run, “transitional” Submariner Date produced between the late 16800 and the early 16610. It is visually near‑identical to late 16800s (gloss dial/white‑gold surrounds) but introduces Rolex’s higher‑grade **904L** stainless steel to the Submariner line—hence collectors’ interest. It retains the **cal. 3035** movement. [Craft & Tailored Journal+2Everest Bands+2](https://journal.craftandtailored.com/the-crossroads-of-vintage-and-contemporary-the-reference-16800-rolex-submariner/)

**Introduction & production.** Introduced around **mid‑1987**, produced for roughly **7–9 months** into **1988**, then replaced by the **16610** in 1989. (There are credible auction examples with late‑1986 serials—see “Serial numbers/eras” for nuance.) [Craft + Tailored+2WatchProSite+2](https://www.craftandtailored.com/products/copy-of-1982-rolex-submariner-ref-16800-transitional-model)

**Key technical specs (as delivered).**

- **Case:** 40 mm steel (**904L**), ~13 mm thick; 300 m/1000 ft water resistance; Triplock crown; unidirectional elapsed‑time bezel with aluminum insert. Sapphire crystal with cyclops. [Craft & Tailored Journal+2Bobswatches.com+2](https://journal.craftandtailored.com/the-crossroads-of-vintage-and-contemporary-the-reference-16800-rolex-submariner/)
- **Movement:** Rolex **calibre 3035** (automatic, quick‑set date, hacking), 28,800 vph, **27 jewels**, free‑sprung Microstella balance with **Breguet overcoil**; power reserve ~**50 h** (see note in Movement section). [American Watchmakers Institute+2WatchBase.com+2](https://www.awci.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/3035-3055-Tech.pdf)
- **Dial:** Black **gloss** with applied **white‑gold** surrounds; **tritium** luminous (“**SWISS‑T<25**” layout). “Spider” (lacquer‑checking) aging is commonly seen on period gloss dials. [Craft & Tailored Journal+2Millenary Watches+2](https://journal.craftandtailored.com/the-crossroads-of-vintage-and-contemporary-the-reference-16800-rolex-submariner/)
- **Bezel/Insert:** Steel bezel with black aluminum insert; period fonts vary (late thin/sans‑serif and later service inserts common). [Bobswatches.com+1](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/watch-101/rolex-submariner-bezels-ultimate-guide.html)
- **Bracelet:** Oyster **93150** with **593** end‑links; fliplock clasp with dive extension. [Rolliworks LLC](https://www.rolliworks.com/shop/20MM-Rolex-SS-93150-Oyster-593-End-pieces-Submariner-16800-168000-B026-p638454569)

**Why this reference matters.** The 168000 is the **first Submariner in 904L steel**, bridging late 16800 (sapphire/300 m/3035) and early 16610 (3135). Its **brief production window** and material change make it a **key transitional reference** prized by collectors. (Rolex had tested 904L earlier on other models; see note in “Verification” below.) [Bobswatches.com+1](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-submariner-168000-904l-stainless-steel.html)

## Bezel / Insert / External Variants

- **Insert types:** Period **aluminum** inserts vary by font. Late‑1980s examples typically show **thin/sans‑serif** numerals; earlier **fat‑font/serif** inserts (more associated with early 16800) are sometimes found from parts swapping or early stock. Modern **service inserts** have distinct modern fonts and luminous (Luminova) pearls. [Bobswatches.com+2WatchProSite+2](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/watch-101/rolex-submariner-bezels-ultimate-guide.html)
- **Pearl:** Tritium pearls (often dark/amber) vs. **Luminova** service pearls (bright/green glow). [CHRONO SHOP](https://chrono-shop.net/en/inserts-inlays-rolex/17000-rolex-new-submariner-bezel-insert-black-stainless-luminova-1680016800016610-watch-part-b315-16800-181.html)
- **Counterfeit risks:** Aftermarket inserts are common; verify **font geometry**, **triangle/pearl construction**, and **reverse finishing** against known originals. [Rolex Forums](https://www.rolexforums.com/showthread.php?t=831021)

## Hands, Movement & Technical Features

**Hands & furniture.** Standard Submariner **Mercedes** hour/min/sweep set in steel with **tritium** fill to match dial; white‑gold surrounds are on the **markers** (dial), not the hands. Tritium should age consistently; mismatched bright hands on a tritium dial usually indicate **service hands**. [Crown & Caliber Blog](https://blog.crownandcaliber.com/how-to-tell-the-difference-between-modern-rolex-submariners/)

**Date wheel fonts.** Period wheels often have **“open 6/9”** forms; **closed 6/9** wheels appear frequently as later **service parts** (early ’90s onward). Mixed sets (e.g., closed 6 in “26”) are documented. [Tapatalk+1](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/vintagerolexforum/rolex-16800-168000-dial-variation-t249940.html)

**Crown & tube.** **703 Triplock 7 mm** crown with **24‑7030** tube. [CHRONO SHOP+1](https://chrono-shop.net/en/a-r-c-h-i-v-e-s-s-o-l-d/3433-rolex-16610-16800-168000-16610t-14060-14060-m-submariner-daytona-116520-gmt-116710-ref-703-watch-crown-part-triplock-stem.html)

**Calibre 3035 (technical).**

- **Type:** Automatic, date, quick‑set; **stop‑seconds**.
- **Beat rate:** **28,800 vph** (4 Hz).
- **Jewels:** **27**.
- **Oscillator:** Free‑sprung balance, **Microstella** screws; **Breguet overcoil** hairspring; KIF shock.
- **Power reserve:** **~50 hours** in factory literature/WatchBase; some secondary sources cite ~42 h on earlier documentation—expect variance after decades and service state. [American Watchmakers Institute+2WatchBase.com+2](https://www.awci.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/3035-3055-Tech.pdf)

## Authentication & Restoration Checklist

**Originality checks (quick list)**

- **Between‑lug reference:** Clean, **factory 168000** stamp at 12; serial at 6. Inspect font and surfaces for signs of **re‑stamping** (added “0”). [Tapatalk](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/vintagerolexforum/168000-t257616.html)
- **Inside case‑back:** Either **168000** or **16800** can be correct on 168000 (documented). Look for period‑appropriate dates/marks. [Christie's+1](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-6424479)
- **Dial:** Gloss WG/tritium with **“SWISS‑T<25”**; period printing traits; spidering acceptable aging. Be cautious of later **“SWISS”** (1998) or **“SWISS MADE”** (1999+) service dials. [Craft & Tailored Journal+1](https://journal.craftandtailored.com/the-crossroads-of-vintage-and-contemporary-the-reference-16800-rolex-submariner/)
- **Hands:** Tritium tone should broadly match dial; bright white hands on an aged tritium dial suggest replacements. [Crown & Caliber Blog](https://blog.crownandcaliber.com/how-to-tell-the-difference-between-modern-rolex-submariners/)
- **Bezel insert:** Period aluminum with appropriate fonts; **Luminova** pearl indicates service; compare fonts to known late‑80s styles. [Bobswatches.com](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-blog/watch-101/rolex-submariner-bezels-ultimate-guide.html)
- **Bracelet:** **93150/593** pairing; verify clasp date (**L/M** for 1987/88) relative to serial. [Rolliworks LLC+1](https://www.rolliworks.com/shop/20MM-Rolex-SS-93150-Oyster-593-End-pieces-Submariner-16800-168000-B026-p638454569)
- **Movement:** **3035** signed & numbered; check condition of rotor axle, autowind reversers, and date mechanism; rate/reserve within spec. [American Watchmakers Institute](https://www.awci.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/3035-3055-Tech.pdf)

**Common red flags**

- **Re‑engraved reference** (added “0” to a 16800). [Tapatalk](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/vintagerolexforum/168000-t257616.html)
- **Service dial/hand set** (Luminova/SL mismatched to tritium case era). [Millenary Watches](https://millenarywatches.com/rolex-swiss-only-dial/)
- **Modern service insert** or aftermarket insert passed as original. [Rolex Forums](https://www.rolexforums.com/showthread.php?t=831021)
- **Over‑polished case** (rounded crown guards, thinned bevels) compromising geometry and value.

**Recommended restoration practices**

- **Sympathetic**: retain original tritium dial/hands if stable; avoid relume. Keep original insert and crystal if serviceability/safety allow.
- **Correct crystal**: **B25‑295‑CAR‑C1** is period‑correct if replacement is required. [paulswatchrepair.com](https://www.paulswatchrepair.com/product/nos-genuine-rolex-sapphire-crystal-b25-295-car-c1/)
- **Correct crown/tube**: **24‑703 / 24‑7030**. Replace gaskets/O‑rings at service. [CHRONO SHOP](https://chrono-shop.net/en/crowns-stems-tubes/5830-rolex-submariner-steel-case-tube-24-7030-0-70mm-fits-on-5512-5513-1680-16800-168000-16610-16610lv-116710-16520.html)
- **Service interval**: 5–10 years depending on use; pressure‑test after any intervention affecting case integrity.
- **Provenance**: retain **bracelet/clasp codes**, **service invoices**, and any **retailer signatures** (e.g., **Tiffany & Co.**) or **COMEX provenance**; these materially affect value. [Collector Square+1](https://www.collectorsquare.com/en/watches/rolex/submariner-date/ref-rolex-168000/lpi)

## Summary

The **Rolex Submariner ref. 168000** is the **material bridge** between the sapphire/300 m **16800** and the **16610** movement upgrade: it keeps the **3035** heartbeat and late‑16800 **gloss tritium** aesthetic, but introduces **904L steel** to the Submariner case. Made for **well under a year (1987–1988)**, it exhibits typical late‑’80s components (93150/593, thin‑font inserts, gloss WG dials with possible “spider” aging) and shows **documented overlap** in case‑back references. Co‑branded (**COMEX**, **Tiffany & Co.**) examples exist and command premiums. For authentication and restoration, focus on **correct 168000 mid‑case engraving**, **tritium dial/hand set**, **3035** correctness, and appropriate bracelet/clasp pairing. [Collector Square+3Bobswatches.com+3WatchProSite+3](https://www.bobswatches.com/rolex-submariner-168000-904l-stainless-steel.html)

### Verification note (conflicting points reconciled)

- **Production window.** Most scholarly/dealer sources place ref. 168000 from **mid‑1987 to 1988** (~7–9 months). However, **Sotheby’s** has catalogued 168000s with **1986** serials (e.g., 9.36M), which likely reflects **non‑sequential case usage** and parts overlap during the switchover. This dossier therefore treats **1987–1988** as the primary production span, with **documented 1986‑serial outliers**. [Craft + Tailored+1](https://www.craftandtailored.com/products/copy-of-1982-rolex-submariner-ref-16800-transitional-model)
- **Power reserve (cal. 3035).** WatchBase & several modern write‑ups give **~50 h**; older material and some dealer notes cite **~42 h**. Practical performance varies with service state; we present **~50 h** as nominal per WatchBase/modern literature and note the variance. [WatchBase.com+1](https://watchbase.com/rolex/caliber/3035)
- **904L “firsts.”** The **168000 is the first Submariner** in 904L; some sources note the **Sea‑Dweller** used 904L earlier (c. **1985**). Both statements can be true; the distinction here is “first **Submariner** in 904L.” [Millenary Watches](https://millenarywatches.com/when-did-rolex-change-to-904l-steel/)
